Flipper NFC Helper

Make a tag here, then emulate it on your Flipper.

Android phones show text tags. iPhones ignore them.

Android phones can join from a Wi-Fi tag. iPhones ignore them.

Android phones offer to save the contact. iPhones ignore contact tags.

Send to Flipper app

Opens the Flipper app: Save, then Emulate.

or

  1. On the Flipper, emulate an NTAG215 (NFC → Saved, or Add Manually).
  2. Tap the button and hold your phone to the Flipper's back.
  3. Press Back on the Flipper to keep it.

Saved

    How it works
    1. Install the free Flipper Mobile App and pair it with your Flipper over Bluetooth (once).
    2. Fill in the form and tap Send to Flipper app.
    3. In the Flipper app, tap Save, then Emulate.
    4. Tap any phone to the back of the Flipper.

    In Chrome on Android you can also skip the Flipper app: Write to Flipper over NFC writes straight to a Flipper that is emulating a tag.

    Nothing you type leaves your phone. The tag data travels in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server.
